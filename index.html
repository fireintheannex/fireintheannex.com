Who We Are

Fire in the Annex (aka FITA) is a powerhouse of musical talent that blends passion with electrifying performances. Fronted by charismatic lead vocalist, Erica Taylor, and back-stopped by veteran touring drummer Ulises Negrete, the band sets the stage on fire with sing-a-long anthems and dance floor vibes. From Heart to Led Zeppelin to My Chemical Romance — FITA dives headfirst into bold choices and tight sets that keep audiences rocking!

Meet The Band

Formed in 2024 to play a one-night-only high school reunion, the chemistry was too hot to ignore. A 'name the band' contest winner, inspired by the high-school's actual annex fire in the 80's, lit the flame and FITA was born!

ERICA TAYLOR

Erica brings versatility, charisma, and a genuine passion for music to every performance. As FITA’s lead vocalist, she delivers on everything from rock anthems to pop favorites with a seemingly effortless style and confidence, while also contributing powerful backup vocals.

Her performance experience includes appearances on the acclaimed stages of CAP21 at NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts, as well as a stint with The Jersey Girls that earned her a spot on the reality TV series Making the Band. Erica has previously toured the New Jersey circuit as a backup singer, but with a voice like hers, she belongs front and center with FITA — where she truly shines.

ULISES NEGRETE

Ulises toured internationally before settling in the U.S. In Cancun, he routinely played large venues such as Daddy Rock, Coco Bongo, Tequila Boom and Hard Rock Café. He has shared the stage with Bob Weir (Grateful Dead), Ronnie Wood (Rolling Stones), and Dave Hlubek (Molly Hatchet), and recorded in studio with George Lynch (Dokken, Lynch Mob). He is currently writing original music with Liquid Island.

DAVE ALPER

Though a classically trained and degreed musician and trumpet player, Dave found his true home on lead guitar. After decades tearing it up in cover bands, he brings a versatile style rooted in prog rock, blues, and folk, while also helping guide the band’s musical direction.

GLENN DEMKOW

Holding down the low end, Glenn is a multi-instrumentalist with decades of live experience. From late-night boardwalk gigs to packed Jersey Shore bars, he's played in numerous bands — most notably as the rhythm guitarist for the Jersey Shore favorite, Upside.

JEFF ENTIN

Keeping the groove on rhythm, Jeff is an aficionado of classic rock, R&B, grunge, and pop-emo, performing in numerous acoustic and electric collaborations. He helps organize an annual Battle of the Bands fundraiser supporting environmental scholarships, where he performs with his Lather Rinse Repeat project.
